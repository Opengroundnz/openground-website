The year

Idea to income, in four terms.

  1. Learn the fundamentalsHow business actually works. Finish with an idea worth chasing.
  2. Meet your mentor. Make your first sale.Paired with a real operator, then take it to market.
  3. Grow it. Hear from guest speakers.More customers, more revenue, and operators on stage who have done it before.
  4. Show itShowcase what you have built over the year, then prize giving. Parents welcome.

The office

Your office is a real one.
