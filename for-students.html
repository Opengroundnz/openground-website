For students

How Openground works.

One year. One real business. Your name on it.

Open to Year 12 students at Westlake Boys, Westlake Girls, Rosmini College and Carmel College from 2027. One cohort, learning together at the B:HIVE, Smales Farm.

The year

Term by term.

01
Term 1

Learn the fundamentals

How business actually works: spotting opportunities, market research, and building an MVP. You finish the term with an idea worth chasing, ready for mentor matching.

02
Term 2

Get your mentor. Make your first sale.

You are paired with a mentor with genuine real-world business experience. Together you build the thing, take it to market, and make your first real sale.

03
Term 3

Grow it

More customers, more revenue, a sharper pitch. Guest speakers run right through the year: founders, investors and innovators who have walked the path before you.

04
Term 4

Showcase Night and prize giving

Pitch what you built to a full room at Showcase Night, hear from a guest speaker, and celebrate the year at the prize giving. Parents welcome.

Joining

Getting in is simple.
Getting through is the challenge.

Registering your interest is deliberately easy. No business idea needed, no long forms. The selection happens later, and Term 1 exists to help you find the idea.

  1. Register your interestTwo minutes. Your details and a caregiver email.
  2. Application details arrive by emailEverything you need to apply, sent to you and your caregiver.
  3. A short video applicationWho you are and what you want to build. No polish needed.
  4. Hear back in Term 4Accepted students are notified and invoiced on acceptance, ready to start in 2027.

Questions

What students and parents ask.

Do I need a business idea already?
No. Term 1 exists to help you find one. Plenty of students arrive with nothing but curiosity.
When and where does it run?
After school at the B:HIVE, Smales Farm, Takapuna. Sessions run Monday to Thursday, 3:30 to 5pm, across the school year.
Who can apply?
From 2027, Year 12 students at Westlake Boys, Westlake Girls, Rosmini College and Carmel College. All four schools learn together as one cohort.
What does it cost?
$1,500 for the year, invoiced on acceptance. It covers the full programme. Scholarships are available: if cost is the only thing stopping you, come and talk to us.
Is the money students make real?
Completely. Students sell to real customers and keep building from there. Some ventures have grown into companies that outlasted school.

Year 13 continues at Westlake Boys High School in 2027. It runs as a course during school time, with full details announced during 2027.

The course

Step by step.

01
Step 1

Start Learning

Create a business plan, conduct market research, and develop an MVP (minimum viable product). You’ll take real, actionable steps to bring your business idea to life.

02
Step 2

Get Paired With a Mentor

You’ll be matched with a mentor who has genuine real-world business experience. They’ll guide you through the ups and downs of building your business and help you navigate challenges along the way.

03
Step 3

Hear From Expert Guest-Speakers

Throughout the programme, you’ll hear from leading entrepreneurs, investors, and innovators who have walked the path before you. These sessions will inspire you, expose you to new industries, and give you valuable insights that most students never get while still in school.

04
Step 4

Pitch For Funding In The Dragon’s Den

Put your ideas to the test in front of real investors, business leaders, and mentors. The Dragons’ Den is your chance to pitch for real funding to take your business to the next level, just like the entrepreneurs you see on TV.

05
Step 5

Go to Market

Once your product or service is ready, it’s time to launch. You’ll take your business to market, start making sales, and experience what it’s really like to run something of your own. This is where everything you’ve learned comes together: strategy, communication, resilience, and entrepreneurial thinking in action.
